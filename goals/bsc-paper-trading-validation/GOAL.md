# BSC paper trading validation

Goal ID: goal_94922181b829
Goal slug: bsc-paper-trading-validation

## Description
Validates the BSC memecoin trading method on paper only, with no real money, wallet use, preview, transaction, or signing. It uses a hypothetical $1,000 bankroll, 1% risk per trade, full gates and money-flow confirmation, and promotes to micro-live only after at least 100 closed trades and 30 days with positive net expectancy and a profit factor above 1.5.
